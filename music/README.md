# Background music

Drop 1-5 royalty-free tracks here (.mp3/.wav) and the pipeline will randomly
pick one per video, loop it, duck it under the narration, and fade it out.

Where to get free, monetization-safe tracks:
- YouTube Audio Library (studio.youtube.com -> Audio Library) — filter by
  "Attribution not required". These are explicitly safe for monetized videos.
- Recommended vibes for this niche: dark ambient, cinematic tension, low drones.

Do NOT use random tracks from the internet — a single Content ID claim can
redirect all the revenue of a video to the rights holder.
